## Stocks News Report
### Q1 Earnings and Financial Updates

#### Mobavenue Ai Tech
The company's revenue has seen a significant increase, rising to ₹728 million in Q1 compared to ₹464 million in the same quarter last year. This represents a year-over-year (YoY) growth of 57%.

#### Mardia Samyoung
The board of directors has approved the unaudited financial results for Q1, which ended on June 2026. The company has also received feedback from its auditors and plans to relocate its corporate office to Ahmedabad on August 11, 2026. A board meeting was held on the same day, with discussions taking place between 8-9 PM.

#### Lumax Auto Technologies
The company's Q1 net profit has seen a substantial increase, rising by 109.2% YoY to ₹86.6 crore. This represents a significant growth in the company's financial performance.

#### Gujjubhai Industries Ltd
The MD (Managing Director) of the company, Paresh Thakker, has submitted his resignation, effective August 10, 2026. A board meeting was held on August 11, 2026, where discussions took place regarding the company's future direction and management.
